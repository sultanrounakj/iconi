Common Questions

Pricing FAQs

Do prices include hosting and domain?
No — hosting and domain are paid directly by you to your provider. We'll recommend the right setup for your project and set everything up on your behalf at no extra charge.
What if I need something in between packages?
Packages are guides, not boxes. If your project sits between Starter and Business, or needs a mix of features, tell us what you need and we'll scope it precisely with a custom quote.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Business and Advanced projects are usually split into 40% to start, 40% at design approval, 20% at launch. Larger projects can be broken into more milestones on request.
How long does delivery take?
Starter: 1–2 weeks. Business: 3–6 weeks. Advanced / Custom: 6–12 weeks depending on scope. You'll get a firm timeline before we start, and regular progress updates throughout.
What happens after launch?
Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support (3 months on Advanced) to fix any issues that arise. After that, ongoing maintenance plans are available if you'd like us to keep things updated, backed up and secure.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes — we work with startups and businesses across the US, UK, EU, Middle East, Australia and India. Invoices can be raised in USD, GBP, EUR or INR. All communication is async-friendly and timezone-flexible.
What if I'm not happy with the design?
Each package includes revision rounds, and we review designs with you in the browser before building. If something isn't right, we fix it. Projects have never gone to litigation — that's not how we work.
