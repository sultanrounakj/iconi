4. Proposals & Agreements

Every project begins with a written proposal or statement of work. This will typically include:

  • Project scope and deliverables.
  • Timeline and milestones.
  • Fixed price or pricing structure.
  • Payment schedule.
  • Revision rounds included.
  • Any assumptions or client dependencies.

A project officially begins when you have accepted the proposal in writing (email confirmation is sufficient) and paid any required deposit. Until then, no work is committed or owed.

5. Project Scope

Every project is scoped in the proposal. The following rules apply:

  • Work outside the agreed scope is treated as additional work and will be quoted separately.
  • Additional work proceeds only with your written approval.
  • Adding pages, features or integrations after the project starts changes the timeline and price.
  • Significant changes in direction after a milestone is signed off may require re-scoping or restarting that milestone.

We will always tell you clearly whether a request counts as a revision (included) or additional work (quoted separately).

6. Payments

Standard payment terms are:

  • Starter projects: 50% deposit to begin, 50% on delivery.
  • Business projects: 40% to begin, 40% at design approval, 20% at launch.
  • Advanced / Custom projects: milestone-based payments defined in the proposal, usually 30–40% at kickoff.

Payment methods and currency are specified in your proposal. We accept bank transfer, UPI, Razorpay and international payment options (for clients outside India).

Late payments: invoices unpaid beyond 15 days of the due date may pause active work. Invoices unpaid beyond 30 days may be subject to a 2% monthly late fee, at our discretion.

7. Revisions

Each project package includes a defined number of revision rounds:

  • Starter: 1 revision round.
  • Business: 3 revision rounds.
  • Advanced / Custom: unlimited revisions within the agreed project scope.

A "revision round" is a consolidated set of feedback on a milestone (design, layout, copy, etc.). We ask you to gather your team's feedback into one batch rather than sending it piecemeal — this keeps the project moving.

Revisions do not extend to changes in the original scope, direction or deliverables.

8. Timelines & Delays

Timelines in your proposal are estimates based on the assumption that:

  • You will provide all required content, brand assets and information promptly.
  • You will respond to feedback requests and milestone approvals within 3 working days.

If these conditions are not met, the timeline will be extended accordingly. We are not liable for delays caused by missing content, late feedback or scope changes.

12. Cancellation Policy

You may cancel a project at any time by written notice (email is sufficient). The following rules apply:

Cancellation by the client

  • Before kickoff (before the deposit is paid and work begins): no charges apply. Any proposal is simply withdrawn.
  • After kickoff but before the first milestone is delivered: you will be charged for work completed up to that point, based on the project's payment schedule.
  • At any milestone: you may cancel at the end of any milestone by notifying us in writing. You pay only for milestones delivered and approved up to that point.
  • Mid-milestone: if you cancel partway through an active milestone, that milestone is charged in full (since work has already begun), and subsequent milestones are cancelled.

Cancellation by iCONI Agency

We reserve the right to cancel or pause a project if:

  • Payment obligations are not met after 30 days past due.
  • Required content, feedback or assets are not provided after repeated requests, causing significant delay.
  • The project becomes impractical due to circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Effect of cancellation

Upon cancellation, we will hand over all completed deliverables produced up to the cancellation date, along with any relevant documentation. Any amounts already paid for work not yet completed will be refunded according to the Refund Policy below.

13. Refund Policy

We want every client to feel the value they paid for. This Refund Policy explains exactly when, how and how much we refund — so there are no surprises for either side.

13.1 Principles behind our refunds

  • No refunds for work already delivered and approved. If you've signed off on a milestone, that work is complete and paid for.
  • Fair cancellation is always refundable. If you cancel before work has started, you get your money back — no questions.
  • We fix issues rather than refund them. If something isn't right, our first priority is to fix it (within the revision rounds included in your package).
  • Refunds are proportional. You only ever pay for what has actually been delivered.

13.2 Refunds by project stage

  • Before kickoff: Full refund of any deposit paid, minus any third-party costs already incurred on your behalf (e.g. domain registration, licence purchase). If no third-party costs have been incurred, the refund is 100%.
  • During the discovery phase (first week): Refund of the deposit minus a fair charge for discovery work completed. Discovery work is typically billed at a pro-rated amount specified in your proposal.
  • During the design phase (before development begins): Refund of the deposit minus the design work completed and delivered. You keep all design files produced up to that point.
  • During the development phase (after design approval): Refund of any deposit amount not yet applied to work completed. You receive all code, design assets and documentation produced up to the cancellation date.
  • After final delivery: No refund is available. The project has been delivered, reviewed and (typically) signed off. Post-launch issues are covered by the Warranty & Support section below.

13.3 Non-refundable items

The following costs are non-refundable, regardless of cancellation stage:

  • Third-party costs already incurred — domain registration, hosting already purchased, paid plugin or theme licences, premium fonts, stock image licences, API subscription fees.
  • Payment gateway and transaction fees — the processing fees charged by Razorpay, Paytm, Stripe or banks when you paid us are not refundable, since they are charged to us by the provider.
  • Completed milestone work — anything already delivered and approved.
  • Rush fees and priority charges — if you paid extra for expedited delivery, those charges are non-refundable once work has begun.
  • Consulting and audit hours — hourly consulting, technical audits and strategy sessions once delivered.

13.4 How to request a refund

To request a refund, email hello@iconi.in with the subject line "Refund Request — [Your Project Name]" and include:

  • Your full name and the project or invoice reference.
  • The reason for the refund request.
  • The amount you believe is fair, and how you arrived at it.

We acknowledge every refund request within 2 working days and give you a formal decision within 7 working days.

13.5 Processing time

Approved refunds are processed as follows:

  • Decision: within 7 working days of receiving your request.
  • Initiation: within 3 working days of approval.
  • Credited to you: 5–10 working days depending on your bank or payment provider. Razorpay and UPI refunds usually appear within 5 working days. International bank transfers may take up to 14 working days.

Refunds are issued to the same payment method you used, unless you specifically request a different one in writing.

13.6 Exceptions

We may decline a refund request if:

  • The work has already been delivered and approved at a milestone.
  • The refund request is made more than 60 days after final project delivery.
  • The client has not participated in the revision process offered (we always give you a chance to fix issues first).
  • The project was cancelled by us due to non-payment, breach of terms or client misconduct.
  • The refund request is for a portion of work already used commercially by the client (e.g. a live website built with our code).

13.7 Disputes

If you disagree with our refund decision, we encourage you to:

  • Ask for a written explanation of how the decision was reached — we'll always provide one.
  • Request a mediation call with a senior team member.
  • Propose an alternative resolution (partial refund, additional work, extended support).

We've never taken a refund dispute to litigation, and we don't intend to start. Our aim is always a fair outcome both sides can live with.
