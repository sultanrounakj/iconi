Common Questions

Before you reach out

Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes — most of our clients are actually outside India. We work across the US, UK, EU, Middle East and Australia. All communication is async-friendly and timezone-flexible. Invoices can be raised in USD, GBP, EUR or INR.
How quickly can you start?
For most projects, we can start within 3–5 days of accepting a proposal. Landing page projects can often begin within 48 hours if the brief is clear.
What information should I include in my enquiry?
The more detail, the better — but don't overthink it. Useful things: what you do, what the site needs to achieve, any reference sites you like, target launch date, and a budget range if you have one. If you don't have all of that, we'll help you figure it out.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, we're happy to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion if that matters to you. Just mention it in your enquiry.
What if I don't know what I need?
That's completely fine. Many of our best projects start that way. Describe the business problem, not the technical solution — we'll recommend the right approach and give you an honest cost breakdown.
